FAQ

Questions patients ask us

Ask your own question
How much does a check-up cost?
Consultation starts from ₱500. It includes a full exam and a written treatment plan with prices, so you know the cost before any treatment.
Do you accept my HMO?
We accept most major HMOs. Bring your HMO card and a valid ID, and we'll check your coverage before any treatment. You can also message us your HMO name in advance.
Can I pay in installments?
Yes, for bigger treatments like braces and implants. You pay a down payment and the rest in up to 12 monthly payments.
What should I bring to my first visit?
A valid ID, your HMO card if you have one, and a list of any medicines you take. If you have old dental X-rays, bring them too.
Do you treat children?
Yes. We see kids from their first teeth. Short, gentle first visits help them get comfortable with the dentist.
Do you accept walk-ins?
Yes, when the schedule allows. Booking ahead by Messenger, Viber or phone means you won't wait.
