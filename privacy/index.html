Privacy Notice

How we handle your personal information

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Ngiti Dental Studio ("Ngiti Dental Studio", "we") operates a dental clinic at 2F Medical Arts Building, 123 Jupiter Street, Bel-Air, Makati City. We respect your privacy and process personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

What we collect through this website

  • Your name and mobile number.
  • Your preferred contact channel, date and time.
  • The service you are interested in, your HMO (if any), and any note you choose to send us.

Please do not send detailed medical history through the website form. We will ask for it in person during your visit.

Why we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request, schedule and confirm your appointment, and contact you about that appointment. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Legal basis

We process your information based on your consent, which you give by ticking the consent box before sending the form, and as needed to take steps you requested before receiving dental services.

Who can see it

Only authorized clinic staff. Our website and form are hosted by service providers acting on our instructions (Cloudflare (website hosting) and Google Workspace (email)), who are bound to protect the information.

How long we keep it

Appointment requests that do not become a visit are deleted within 6 months. If you become our patient, your records are kept as required for dental and medical records.

Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access, correct, object to processing, have your data erased or blocked, data portability, to withdraw consent, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). To use any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer.
